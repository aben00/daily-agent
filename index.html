How it works

1
Hold a Binance Agent ID
Any name.ai identity works — the contract checks it on-chain.
2
Check in once a day
One check-in per UTC day, straight from your wallet. Gas only.
3
Collect your badge NFT
Each check-in mints a free badge. Check in on consecutive days to build your streak.
The badge is just proof you showed up. The contract has no owner, no fees and no pause switch.

Your wallet

Connect with MetaMask or the Binance Wallet in-app browser. No QR codes, no sessions stored anywhere.
