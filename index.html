DailyAgent

Daily check-in badges for Agent ID holders.

How it works

Hold a Binance Agent ID (name.ai) and check in once per UTC day. Each check-in mints a free badge NFT to your wallet. Check in on consecutive days to build your streak.
Free — gas only. No token, no rewards, no earnings. The badge is just proof you showed up. The contract has no owner, no fees and no pause switch.
